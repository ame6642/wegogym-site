1. Who we are

WeGoGym is run by Amman Meah, a sole trader, trading as WeGoGym. He is the controller of your personal information, which means he decides how and why it is used and is responsible for protecting it.

For anything about your personal information, email hello@wegogym.club.

We do not use your information for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not make decisions about you by automated means alone.

When you email us

We keep your message and our reply so we can deal with what you asked. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering you.

When you visit the site

Like any website, the services that deliver this site briefly receive technical information such as your IP address and browser type, and may keep it in short-lived logs for security. When you log in to register a facility, our account provider also records your IP address and browser for security. See section 4.

3. What is made public

Your facility’s listing is only shown to the public once we have approved it. At that point its name, address, type, description, opening hours, amenities and photos are visible to anyone. Your personal details, date of birth, registered business details and contact details are never shown on the listing.

Photos are stored in a public file store as soon as you upload them, including before approval, so someone who knows how to look could find them. Please only upload photos you are happy to be public, and do not upload photos that show people who have not agreed to it.

4. Who we share it with

We use these service providers to run WeGoGym. Except where noted, they handle information only on our instructions, under contract.

  • Supabase: our database, accounts, file storage and server code. Data is stored in Supabase’s London data centre.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website itself. GitHub keeps its own security logs, which include visitors’ IP addresses, under its own privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts: supplies the website’s fonts, so Google receives your IP address and browser details when a page loads. Google handles this under its own privacy policy, not on our instructions.
  • Google Workspace (Google Sheets): a working copy of waitlist sign-ups. If you ask us to delete your waitlist entry, we delete it from this copy too.
  • Resend: sends our emails, such as account codes and launch updates.
  • Zoho Mail: our email inbox, which holds messages you send us.

We will also share information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of WeGoGym, our users or others.

5. Information stored outside the UK

Our main database is in the UK. Some of the providers above are based in the United States or elsewhere and may handle information there. Where that happens we rely on the UK’s legal safeguards for international transfers: UK adequacy regulations (including the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, for providers certified under it) or the UK’s approved contract terms for transfers. You can ask us for details.

6. How long we keep it

  • Waitlist: until WeGoGym launches plus 12 months, or sooner if you ask us to delete it or unsubscribe.
  • Unfinished facility registrations: deleted after 12 months with no activity. If WeGoGym is not open in your country yet, we keep your draft until we open there, or until you ask us to delete it.
  • Facility accounts and listings: while the account is open. After it closes we keep business and tax records for up to 6 years, as the law requires, and delete the rest.
  • Emails with us: up to 2 years after the conversation ends, unless they form part of business records.
  • Security logs held by our providers: for their usual short periods.

7. Cookies and browser storage

This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. If you log in to register a facility, your browser stores a login token, with your account’s email address and name, so you stay logged in and can come back to your registration. It is removed when you log out, so log out when you use a shared computer. This is needed for the service you asked for, so it does not need your consent.

8. Keeping it safe

Information travels over encrypted connections. Access to it is limited by database rules, so each facility account can only see and change its own registration, and only WeGoGym administrators can see registrations as a whole. If something goes wrong that puts your information at risk, we will tell you and the regulator where the law requires it.

9. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete your information;
  • limit how we use it, or object to how we use it;
  • give you the information you provided in a common electronic format, or send it to someone else.

Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time, for example by unsubscribing from the waitlist. This does not affect anything we did before you withdrew it.

To use any of these rights, email hello@wegogym.club. We will reply within one month. We may ask you to confirm who you are first. There is normally no charge.

10. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your personal information, you have the right to complain to us. Email hello@wegogym.club with “Data protection complaint” in the subject. We will acknowledge it within 30 days, look into it, keep you updated and tell you the outcome.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), the UK data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or by phone on 0303 123 1113.

11. Children

The waitlist is not intended for children under 13, and you must be 18 or over to register a facility. If you think a child has given us their information, email us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this notice

We will update this notice when what we do with personal information changes, for example when bookings open. The date at the top shows when it last changed. If a change affects you significantly, we will tell you by email.
