River Run

The Marquis of Wellington Every Tuesday, Thursday and Sunday

Free group runs along the Thames, three times a week, from Bermondsey.

When

  • Every Tuesday6:15pmAll paces. Easy group (about 6:30 per km) leaves at 6:45pm.
  • Every Thursday6:15pmAll paces and routes.
  • Every Sunday10:30amRoutes head east. No bag drop on Sundays.

About this event

Weekday runs head west along the river to Millennium Bridge (about 6.5k), Blackfriars Bridge (8k) or Westminster Bridge (11k) and back. Sunday runs head east: about 6.5k, 10k to Greenland Pier, or 18k through the Greenwich foot tunnel and back over Tower Bridge. Social membership is free: make a profile on the club's website, then turn up.
